What the price does not include

These are third-party costs. They are paid to the providers, not to us, and we will always tell you the amount before anything is charged.

AI usage

Image, video and voice generation are billed by the provider per use. Heavy production means higher usage.

Hosting & domains

Static hosting can be free; a custom domain is an annual cost paid directly to the registrar.

Paid tools & APIs

Any subscription or API tier needed for your workflow is yours, in your name, at list price.

Questions people actually ask

Is ₦50,000 the full cost?

It is the full cost of our setup work. AI usage, hosting and any paid tools are separate, billed at cost, and usually small at the start. We quote them before work begins.

Do I have to take the monthly plan?

No. Setup is standalone. The monthly plan exists for people who want the workflow to keep running without managing it.

What counts as “agreed monthly scope”?

A specific number of videos and tasks written into your scope document during the demo call. It is not unlimited, and we won’t pretend otherwise.

Can I cancel?

Yes — the monthly plan ends at the close of the current billing period after written notice. See the Refund Policy.

What if a platform blocks the automation?

We tell you and stop that task. We don’t work around platform restrictions, and we don’t charge for work we couldn’t deliver.

Want the exact number for your case?

Twenty minutes on a call, and you’ll have a written scope with the third-party costs estimated.
