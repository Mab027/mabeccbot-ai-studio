Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who we are

MabeccBot AI Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates this website and provides AI-assisted content production, publishing automation and workflow services. We are based in Nigeria and work with clients internationally.

MabeccBot AI Studio is a trading name; our business registration details are available on request. For any privacy question, contact talesbeneaththebaobab01@gmail.com.

2. What this website collects

This is a static website. It has no user accounts, no login and no database of its own.

  • Contact form. The form on the Contact page does not send data to our server. It opens a pre-filled message in your own email application, which you decide whether to send. We only receive what you send us.
  • Booking calendar. The Book a Demo page embeds a calendar supplied by Cal.com. When you open or use it, Cal.com may set its own cookies and process your name, email and chosen time under its own privacy policy. We receive the booking details you submit.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider may record standard technical information — such as IP address, browser type and pages requested — for security and performance purposes.
  • Analytics. We do not currently run any website analytics or advertising trackers. If we add analytics later, this policy will be updated first and, where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent.
  • Cookies. This website sets no cookies of its own. Third-party services you choose to use — the booking calendar, or a payment provider once checkout is live — may set cookies when you interact with them.

3. What we collect during client work

When you engage us, we may hold:

  • Your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number and business details
  • Project materials you provide — scripts, images, brand assets, account identifiers
  • Correspondence and invoices
  • Authorisation tokens for platforms you connect. These are stored with restricted access, are never displayed, and can be revoked by you at any time.

4. What we never ask for

We never ask for your platform passwords, and we do not log in to your accounts as you. Where automation is used, it runs through official authorisation that you grant and can withdraw.

5. Why we process your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • To deliver the services you have engaged us for
  • To keep records of work performed and payments made
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the work — for example our hosting provider, our email provider, the booking calendar, a payment processor once checkout is live, and the platforms you ask us to publish to — or where the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are kept for up to 12 months. Project records and invoices are kept for up to 6 years, because tax and accounting rules require it, or longer where the law requires us to keep a record. After that we delete or anonymise them.

8. Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, request a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also object to a particular use. Email us and we will respond within 30 days; if a request is unusually complex, we will tell you why and when to expect an answer.

What we actually do. We collect only the information you send us or supply during a project. We store it on access-restricted systems. We do not sell it. We share it only with the service providers listed in section 6, or where the law requires it. We delete it on the schedule set out in section 7. Where Nigerian or other applicable data protection law gives you rights, we will honour them.

We do not claim certification, registration or regulatory approval, and nothing on this page should be read as a guarantee of compliance with any particular law.

9. Security

Access to client data is limited to the people doing the work. Credentials are stored with restricted file permissions and are never printed in logs or shared in chat messages. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you promptly and explain what happened.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adult creators. This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects you, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell active clients by email.
