Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. The short version

  • Setup fee: refundable in full if you cancel before work begins. If you cancel after work has started, we refund the part of the fee for work not yet carried out — less any third-party costs already incurred on your behalf, and only where those costs are reasonable, were actually spent on your project, and are shown to you.
  • Monthly subscription: if you cancel part-way through a period, we refund the unused part of that period on a pro-rata basis where the service for that time has not been delivered.
  • Third-party costs: these are paid to providers (AI usage, hosting, domains) and are not refundable by us. Any deduction we make for them must be reasonable, actually incurred for your project, and disclosed to you.

2. Setup fee

The ₦100,000 standard setup fee (or ₦50,000 launch price) covers scoping, configuration and production of your first deliverables.

  • Before work begins: full refund on request, no explanation needed.
  • After work begins: we refund the unused portion of the setup fee, calculated on the work completed at the date you cancel. Where the agreed work has been delivered, no refund is due for that completed work.
  • If the work is not as agreed: tell us. We will correct it, or refund the affected amount.
  • If we cannot deliver: if a platform restriction or technical limit means we cannot perform the agreed scope at all, we refund the unused portion of the setup fee.

3. Monthly subscription

  • Billed in advance. If you cancel part-way through a period, we refund the unused part of that period on a pro-rata basis where the service for that time has not been delivered.
  • Cancel in writing at least 7 days before the next billing date and no further charge is made.
  • If we fail to deliver the agreed monthly scope for reasons within our control, we credit the shortfall or refund the pro-rata amount — your choice.

4. Third-party costs

AI usage fees, hosting, domains, paid APIs and similar charges are paid to third parties. They are passed on at cost and are not refundable by us, including where the underlying work is later cancelled. We will always tell you the amount before incurring it.

5. Platform actions are not our liability

We are not responsible for platform decisions — content takedowns, demonetisation, account restrictions or API access being withdrawn. These are outside our control and are not grounds for a refund of work already delivered. We will always tell you honestly when a platform is the limiting factor.

6. How to request a refund

Email talesbeneaththebaobab01@gmail.com with your invoice reference and the reason. We acknowledge within 2 business days and decide within 10 business days.

Approved refunds are returned by the original payment method within 7–14 business days. Any transaction fees charged by the payment provider are deducted where they are not returned to us.

7. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us first — most issues are resolved faster that way. You are free to use your card issuer’s dispute process at any time, and doing so will not, by itself, end our agreement. If a dispute is raised while work is in progress, we may agree with you to pause delivery until it is resolved.

8. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits, excludes or waives any right you have that cannot lawfully be excluded or waived. That includes rights under Nigerian consumer protection law and, where it applies, the consumer law of the country you live in. Where those rights give you more than this policy does, those rights apply.
