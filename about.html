About

We build the pipeline before we sell it.

MabeccBot AI Studio is a small studio working with AI-assisted video, publishing automation and workflow tooling. We started by running the production ourselves — writing, generating, narrating, assembling and publishing — so that what we offer is something we have actually run end to end.

How we work

Every engagement starts with a written scope: what will be produced, how often, and what it costs in third-party fees. If something can’t be automated for your accounts, that goes in the scope too — before you pay.

We use free and low-cost tooling by default, and we tell you where the real costs are.

What we’re building

Our own folklore channel, Tales Beneath the Baobab, is in production with this pipeline: original scripts, AI-assisted visuals, narration, subtitles, chaptered descriptions and thumbnails, delivered through the official YouTube API.

The first film is finished and currently held privately while we complete our review before release. Building it is also where we find the limits — render times, platform quotas, verification requirements — that we pass on to clients instead of hiding.

Principles

✓

Honest limits

If a platform blocks it, we say so. We don’t sell automation that doesn’t exist.

✓

Cost transparency

Our fee and third-party costs are shown separately. You always know what you’re paying for.

✓

Your accounts stay yours

You authorise, you can revoke. We never ask for passwords or take ownership of your channels.
